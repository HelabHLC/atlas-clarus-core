# Third-Party Data and Notices

ATLAS Clarus relies on external colour-reference resources whose licences and provenance must be preserved.

## HLC Colour Atlas XL

The supporting HLC Colour Atlas XL colour-value material is published by freieFarbe e.V. and identifies the colour-value tables as **CC-BY-ND 4.0**.

Do not assume that the licence of this GitHub repository automatically relicenses upstream HLC data.

Before redistributing reference datasets:

- preserve attribution;
- preserve the upstream licence;
- verify whether modification is permitted;
- publish checksums;
- distinguish original upstream files from derived files.

## ICC and FOGRA material

ICC profiles and FOGRA reference material may have their own distribution terms.

Do not add them to a public repository until redistribution rights have been checked.

## ATLAS Clarus code/content licence

No new project-wide licence is declared by this starter package.

A repository licence should be selected only after the owner confirms which parts are intended to be open-source, open-data, documentation-only or proprietary.
